Who We Are

Reimagining Insurance For The Digital Age

We merge modern data tools with proactive risk analysis to structure insurance coverages that are transparent and easy to claim.

Established with a focus on trust and transparent terms, Insurio has grown from a local advisory firm into a premium digital insurance broker. We serve corporate enterprises and private policyholders worldwide, helping navigate complex liabilities with clear, tailored guidelines.

Our consultants prioritize personalized reviews. We check your assets, family demands, and long-term targets to construct optimal shield frameworks that protect without bloated premium costs.

Our Journey

Milestones of Progress

A chronology of our growth, expanding our global network and developing robust digital insurance frameworks.

2001

Foundation In New York

Insurio was founded with five advisors focusing on local home and life insurance consulting, building initial trust.

2008

Commercial Shield Launch

We structured our Commercial Liability team to support mid-size corporations during the financial crisis, offering customized protection.

2016

Digital Desk Initiative

Launched our secure web portal to enable policyholders to adjust terms online and track claims digitally in real-time.

2026

Global Claims Network

Partnered with medical networks across 150+ countries, delivering instantaneous claims processing and support worldwide.
